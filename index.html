Using TSS Viewer

Drag a waveformSideways: shift it along the time axis (or the whole file, see Dragging moves). Up/down: move its level on the plot (display only)
Alt+wheelScale the selected waveform on the plot (display only)
Type a shiftUse the Δt box of a waveform (shown when it is selected or shifted). Accepts 12.5n, -3 us, 1e-9, 40 samples
← / →Nudge the selected waveform by one sample (Shift: 10 samples, Alt: 1 pixel)
↑ / ↓Move the selected waveform up/down by one pixel (Shift: 10)
OverlayIn the selected waveform's row (right), pick In Vd etc. to draw it in another channel's pane, scaled to fit (e.g. dIs/dt over Vd). × and + set its scale and offset; 0 puts it back
Drag backgroundPan time (also Y once the pane's Y range is locked)
WheelZoom time around the mouse
Shift+wheel / wheel on Y axisZoom Y of that pane (locks its Y range)
Drag Y axisPan Y of that pane
Shift+drag, or Box zoom modeZoom to a box (a flat box zooms time only)
Double-click / FFit all waveforms and unlock Y ranges
CToggle cursors A/B; drag them to measure. The box at the top right lists every waveform at A and B, with Δ and Δ/Δt
W / T / DShow the Waveforms, Trends or Double pulse tab
Trends tabFrame trends: mean, integral, min/max… of a channel in a time window, on every frame. Click a point to show that frame in the Waveforms tab (Shift+click adds it). Click a measurement to show its window there
Double pulse tab tick the captures, assign the channels, pick the parameters and Extract. Tick a result (the box in front of its row) to see its waveforms with the extraction points and windows above the table; tick more to overlay them. Dragging a waveform moves its marks with it
ZToggle box-zoom mode
FramesFor FastFrame captures type frames like 1-5, 8 or click the frame buttons

Choose files by test conditions

1. Narrow the list with the value boxes under each condition. 2. Tick the files to load (click a row, or the box in the header for every file shown). 3. Click Load. Click a condition's name to sort by it. Mark name under conditions and click Display names from conditions to label the files with them.

Math channels

A math channel is computed for every loaded file that has its input channels, and appears there as a ƒ channel. Inputs are taken with their own Δt shifts, so deskewing Vd against Is also deskews Vd*Is.

A B
Expression syntax
  • Channels by label (Vd), by scope channel (ch3), or in brackets when the label has other characters ([VGI_S1], [Vd×Is]). Other math channels can be used too.
  • Operators + - * / ^ and parentheses; numbers may use SI suffixes: 2n, 10u, 1k. t is time in seconds.
  • integ(x): running integral over time from the start of the record. Energy between cursors A and B is the Δ shown for integ(Vd*Is).
  • diff(x): time derivative, e.g. diff(Is) in A/s. smooth(x, w): moving average over w samples, or over w seconds when w < 1 (e.g. smooth(diff(Is), 2n)).
  • abs sqrt exp ln log10 min(a,b) max(a,b)
